Energy A.I. Hackathon

Forecasting three-year oil production from well logs

UT PGE Energy A.I. Hackathon 2026 / Jan 30 to Feb 1, 2026 / 260+ participants

A three-day subsurface challenge hosted by UT PGE. The task: predict cumulative three-year oil production for preproduction wells, with an uncertainty model, using well logs, production history and a seismic-derived sand map.

Drawing on my petroleum engineering background, I led feature interpretation for a scikit-learn random forest trained on 71 producing wells: interpreting which well-log and geological features were plausible predictors, and relating the model's outputs back to reservoir and production logic. I mapped production spatially and analyzed gas-oil ratio at peak to investigate patterns and possible drivers of performance across the field.

Downtown Tyler Internship

Where downtown demand is leaking

City of Tyler, Downtown Department / Home to Texas Internship / Jun 1 to Jul 31, 2026

Two months with the City of Tyler working on why local spending was going elsewhere. I analyzed Placer.ai foot-traffic and demographic data across 4,000+ commercial parcels, then ran leakage and void analysis to identify categories with real demand and no business serving it.

The findings informed a developer-recruitment sheet highlighting downtown opportunities and available incentive programs. I independently conducted outreach to 2,000+ contractors to gather input on barriers to downtown investment.
