Engineering Direction

Building depth in petroleum engineering while expanding the tools I can bring to technical problems.

I am a Petroleum Engineering undergraduate at The University of Texas at Austin, studying through the Cockrell School of Engineering and developing toward reservoir engineering as my intended technical direction.

Alongside that engineering foundation, I am building computational capability through work with energy data, Python, SQL, geospatial analysis, and time-series analysis. I am also developing stronger Excel and automation workflows. These tools are not a separate professional identity; they are capabilities I want to use intelligently when an engineering problem benefits from them.

My current experience includes leading data and geospatial work in a UT Austin Energy AI Hackathon, cleaning and auditing 71 complex well logs, and using location intelligence to analyze more than 4,000 business areas during work with the City of Tyler.

The long-term objective is a petroleum and reservoir engineering career supported by strong quantitative judgment, modern analytical tools, and an ability to work across technical and professional environments.
